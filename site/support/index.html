Common questions

None of my busy days are colored.

Dinky Cal needs permission to read your calendars. Open the calendar from the menu bar and click Show My Events. If you said no earlier, turn it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Still nothing? Right-click the menu bar icon, choose Settings…, and check that the calendars you care about are switched on.

Why is every Wednesday bright blue?

Probably an all-day event marked as busy, like a weekly reminder. All-day busy events count as a full day. Mark the event as Free in your calendar app and Dinky Cal will skip it, or switch off its calendar in Settings.

What do the colors mean, exactly?

They measure how many hours of your day are booked. Overlapping events count once; declined invites and events marked free don't count. With the default 9-to-5 day: under 2 hours is the palest circle, then 2–4, 4–6, and 6+ hours (or any all-day event) is the boldest. Change your hours in Settings → Busy hours.

Why are Birthdays and holidays turned off?

They're mostly all-day events, so they'd make lots of days look fully booked. They start unchecked; switch them on in Settings if you want them.

How do I open it with the keyboard?

Set a shortcut in Settings → General → Show calendar. With the calendar open, the arrow keys move between days, ⌘← and ⌘→ change months, t jumps to today, and Esc closes it.

Does it work with Google Calendar or Outlook?

Yes. Add the account in System Settings → Internet Accounts (or in Calendar.app), and Dinky Cal reads it like any other calendar.

Can I add or edit events?

No, on purpose. Click Open Calendar at the bottom of the popover to jump to Calendar.app.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu (right-click the icon → Quit Dinky Cal) and drag it from Applications to the Trash. If you installed with Homebrew: brew uninstall --cask --zap dinkycal.

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
