Dinky Cal

A tiny month calendar behind the date in your Mac's menu bar. The dots show how busy each day is.

    Brighter dot, busier day.

    Dinky Cal counts the hours you're booked, not how many things are on the list. Five quick calls and one long workshop shouldn't look the same.

    A littleunder 2 hours
    Some2 to 4 hours
    A lot4 to 6 hours
    Booked6+ hours or all day
    • Overlaps count once. Two meetings at 10am are one busy hour, not two.
    • Declined and "free" don't count. Neither do Birthdays or holiday feeds, until you say so.
    • Your day, your hours. Busy is measured from 9 to 5 unless you pick other hours.
    • Today doesn't count. Hover next Tuesday on a Tuesday and it says "In 7 days", not 8.

    Click the date. That's the whole app.

    It reads the calendars your Mac already has: iCloud, Google, Exchange, anything in Internet Accounts. No sign-in, no setup.

    • Hover a dayHow far away it is and how busy it is, instantly.
    • Click a dayA ring goes around it and its events list below.
    • Pick your calendarsLeave out the shared family calendar or the garbage-day reminders.
    • Week numbersOptional, down the right side, following your Mac's own week settings.
    • A hotkeySet one and the month pops open from anywhere.
    • Your accent colorDots and today follow whatever color you picked in System Settings.

    Small on purpose.

    It's a month, some dots, and your events. That's it. Things it will never do:

    • Make you sign in
    • Talk to the internet
    • Track anything
    • Nag you to rate it
    • Edit your events
